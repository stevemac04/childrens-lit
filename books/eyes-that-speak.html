Eyes That Speak to the Stars

Picture book · 2022

Eyes That Speak to the Stars

by Joanna Ho · illustrated by Dung Ho

#OwnVoices · Yes Ages 4–8 Taiwanese / Chinese American

Synopsis

A young Asian boy is hurt when a classmate draws him with eyes as flat lines across his face. Comforted by his father, grandfather (Agong), and baby brother, he begins to see his eyes differently — eyes that rise to the skies, speak to the stars, shine like sunlit rays, and carry the stories of those who came before.

This companion to Eyes That Kiss in the Corners centers a boy’s path from shame toward pride, rooted in love between the men in his family.

Why read it?

It names a specific form of racism kids still face — stereotypes about East Asian eyes — without turning the child into a lesson object. The healing comes from family, history, and self-recognition, not from proving himself to the classmate.

Lyrical text and luminous art make it strong for read-alouds about identity, belonging, and whose features get treated as “normal.”
